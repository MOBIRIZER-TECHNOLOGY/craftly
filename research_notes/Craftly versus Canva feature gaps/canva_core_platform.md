## 1. Editor: text, elements, layers, colour, effects, pages, history, shortcuts, bulk create

### Takeaway
Canva's editor covers the full direct-manipulation toolset: text effects (including curve), lists and spacing; frames, grids, tables, charts, connector lines and QR codes; Position/Layers panels, guides, rulers and locking; eyedropper and gradients; border, corner-rounding, shadow and transparency; multi-page with grid view and notes; version history; Bulk Create. Free covers core editing. The gates that matter are Pro for multiple Brand Kits (Free gets 1 kit, colours capped at 3), custom fonts, transparent-background and SVG download, version history and Bulk Create, and Business/Enterprise for scale (100 and 1,000 Brand Kits). The built-in font count and the full shortcut list could not be sourced.

### Cited Findings

**Text and fonts**
- **Brand Kit font upload (custom fonts):** up to 500 uploaded fonts per Brand Kit, up to 18 styles per family; OTF, TTF and WOFF; file under 15 MB; fonts must be licensed for embedding; only Brand Kit admins/owners can upload. Plans named: Pro, Teams/Business, Education, Nonprofit (Brand Kits themselves also exist on Enterprise; whether Enterprise has a different font cap was not confirmed). — [Help: Upload and use Brand Kit fonts](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-fonts/) [S]; formats also in [Help: upload formats and requirements](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-formats-requirements/) [S]
- **Text effects ("Effects" menu):** includes Drop, Glow and Outline among others, with adjustable intensity, colour, direction and transparency. — [Help: Enhance text with effects](https://www.canva.com/help/text-effects/) [S]
- **Curved text ("Curve" effect):** a Curve option in the Effects menu with an adjustable slider. — [Canva feature page: Curved Text](https://www.canva.com/features/curved-text/) [S] and [Help: text effects](https://www.canva.com/help/text-effects/) [S]
- **Lists:** a List button on the text toolbar offers bullet and numbered lists. — [Help: Format text](https://www.canva.com/help/format-text/) [S]
- **Spacing:** Advanced settings > Spacing popup; line spacing defaults to 1.4 and goes down to 0.5; letter spacing defaults to 0. — [Help: Format text](https://www.canva.com/help/format-text/) [S]
- **Text styles in Brand Kit:** Brand Kits store fonts, pairings, sizes and weights, but letter spacing and line spacing cannot be stored in a Brand Kit. — [Help: Brand Kit best practices](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-kit-best-practices/) [S]
- **Edit text semantics (File > Accessibility):** tag text as paragraph, heading 1 to 6 or quote, used for accessible PDFs. — [Help: Edit text semantics](https://www.canva.com/help/edit-text-semantics/) [S]
- **Built-in font library size:** no official count found. Canva's developer docs describe it only as a large library with no number; apps see a limited subset, cannot access Pro fonts, cannot upload custom fonts and cannot download font files; font settings apply to paragraphs, not inline text. — [canva.dev: Using fonts](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/fonts.md) [D]

**Elements: shapes, frames, grids, lines, charts, tables, QR codes, widgets**
- **Elements panel categories:** shapes, lines, frames, grids, tables, charts; some shapes can be converted into frames. — [Help: Borders, frames, and shapes](https://www.canva.com/help/borders-frames-shapes/) [S]; [Design School: Exploring the Element Library](https://www.canva.com/design-school/resources/using-and-editing-elements/) [S]
- **Frames and Grids:** frames are placeholders that crop photos/videos into a shape; grids hold several photo/video slots with adjustable cell spacing; media snaps into the slot when dragged over. — [Help: Use frames](https://www.canva.com/help/using-frames/) [S]; [Help: Use grids](https://www.canva.com/help/using-grids/) [S]
- **Connector lines:** lines can be attached to shapes to build diagrams and flowcharts. — [Help: Use lines to connect shapes](https://www.canva.com/en_gb/help/connect-lines-to-elements/) [S]
- **Tables:** add and delete rows/columns, resize and merge cells, change borders; typing /table on the page inserts one with chosen rows/columns. — [Help: Add and delete tables, rows, and columns](https://www.canva.com/help/adding-and-deleting-tables/) [S]
- **Charts:** pick a chart type in Elements, then Edit to type or import data; chart colours are editable (charts do not follow colour themes automatically). — [Help: Import data into Canva Charts](https://www.canva.com/en_au/help/import-to-charts/) [S]; [Help: Change chart colors](https://www.canva.com/help/change-chart-colors/) [S]
- **Interactive elements:** QR codes, forms and quizzes are listed together as interactive elements (steps not retrieved). — [Help: Elements](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/elements/) [S]
- **Element model exposed to developers:** eight types: Embeds, Images, Groups, Shapes, Tables, Text, Richtext, Videos; the docs say more types will be added later. App elements are groups with metadata. — [canva.dev: Elements](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/elements.md) [D]
- **Widgets:** no source found for a "widgets" element (see Gaps).

**Layers and arrangement**
- **Position panel:** select an element and open Position on the toolbar; alignment is relative to the page for one element and to the selection for several. — [Help: Group, layer, and align elements](https://www.canva.com/help/layer-group-align/) [S]
- **Layers tab:** Position > Layers lists the current page's layers; drag to reorder; Shift to multi-select. — [Help: Finding and arranging layers](https://www.canva.com/help/finding-and-arranging-layers/) [S]
- **Group/ungroup, order, text alignment shortcuts:** Ctrl/Cmd+G group, Ctrl+Shift+G ungroup, Ctrl+] forward, Ctrl+[ backward, Alt+Ctrl+] to front, Shift+Ctrl+L/C/R text left/centre/right. — [Help: Canva keyboard shortcuts](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/) [S]; [Help: Group, layer, and align](https://www.canva.com/help/layer-group-align/) [S]
- **Lock:** Alt+Shift+L; "Lock" fixes position, style and content; "Lock position only" leaves content editable; locked elements cannot be recoloured. — [Help: Lock and unlock pages or elements](https://canva.com/help/article/lock-and-unlock-elements) [S]
- **Rulers and guides:** rulers (pixel units, top and left) work on a computer only; preset guides are 12 columns, 3 columns and 3x3 grid, plus custom guides; guides are locked by default and not exported; Shift+R toggles rulers and guides; margins toggle under File > Rulers and guides. Custom margin sizes are not supported (simulate with guides). — [Help: Use margins, bleed, rulers, and crop marks](https://www.canva.com/help/margins-bleed-crop-marks/) [S]; [Help: Add, unlock, and remove guides](https://www.canva.com/help/adding-and-removing-guides/) [S]; [Help: Page settings, guides, and layers](https://www.canva.com/help/pages-guides-layers/) [S]
- **Snapping:** elements snap to guides/page edges and media snaps into grid cells; a separate snapping toggle was not confirmed. — [Help: Use margins, bleed, rulers](https://www.canva.com/help/margins-bleed-crop-marks/) [S]
- **Docs exception:** margins, bleed, rulers and crop marks are not available in Canva Docs. — [Help: Use margins, bleed, rulers](https://www.canva.com/help/margins-bleed-crop-marks/) [S]

**Colour**
- **Colour panel eyedropper ("Pick a colour"):** hover over the design to sample; "Apply to all pages" replaces a colour everywhere when it is used by more than one element. — [Help: Change element colours](https://www.canva.com/en_au/help/element-color/) [S]
- **Gradients:** usable on text, backgrounds, shapes, table cells, frames and Brand Kits; custom gradients up to 10 colours. — [Help: Create and use colour gradients](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/creating-using-gradients/) [S]
- **Colour themes:** apply a palette across a design; images, charts and locked elements do not update automatically; themes are unavailable in Docs, whiteboards, emails and Sheets (use the colour picker instead). — [Help: Add and apply color themes](https://www.canva.com/help/color-themes/) [S]
- **Brand colours:** Brand Kit palettes appear directly in the colour panel; only admins and brand designers can change them. — [Help: Apply Brand Kit colors](https://www.canva.com/help/apply-brand-kit-colors/) [S]; [Help: brand kit colour palettes](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/color-palettes/) [S]
- **Free plan Brand Kit colours:** Free Brand Kit restricted to 3 colours from 2026-01-19 (tracker log). — [PricingSaaS: Canva plans](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/canva) [D, tracker T]

**Effects**
- **Borders (Stroke style) and corner rounding:** pick a style and weight; Corners control rounds corners by slider or number; frames use a "Corner rounding" control; polygons use Corners for side count and rounding; borders cannot be applied to text boxes, shadows, flipped shapes or some editable shapes; uploaded SVGs cannot have corners/strokes changed. — [Help: Add borders and rounded corners](https://www.canva.com/help/add-borders-round-corners/) [S]
- **Transparency:** toolbar slider or typed value; colour flyout has its own slider for solid fills. — [Help: Adjust element transparency](https://www.canva.com/help/transparency/) [S]
- **Shadows (photos):** Edit > Shadows with styles; adjustable blur, direction, intensity and colour depending on style; "Glow" gives a blurred border. — [Help: Add shadows to photos](https://www.canva.com/help/add-photo-shadows/) [S]
- **Blur as an element effect:** not confirmed in what I could retrieve (photo editing is covered by another researcher).

**Pages**
- **Grid view:** shows all pages at once; drag and drop to reorder. — [Help: Page view settings](https://www.canva.com/help/page-view-settings/) [S]
- **Notes:** a Notes field per page (bottom of editor), shown in Presenter view and Present-and-record; designs can be downloaded with notes. — [Help: Download designs with notes](https://www.canva.com/help/download-notes/) [S]
- **Resize / custom size limits:** minimum 40 x 40 px, maximum 8000 x 3125 px per the Help Center; out-of-range values turn red; workaround is to start from a supported size then resize or lock aspect ratio. — [Help: Resize designs and size limits](https://www.canva.com/help/resize/) [S]. **Conflict:** canva.dev says most designs range from 40 x 40 to 8,000 x 8,000 px. — [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]
- **Page count/length:** most designs allow multiple pages; Docs have unlimited length; Whiteboards have unlimited width and height. — [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]
- **Resize as a plan-gated capability:** the developer docs treat `resize` as a capability that is often tied to the user's plan, with an illustrative trial quota of 2 resize operations for a free user's lifetime. One third-party reviewer lists "Magic Resize" under Pro. — [canva.dev: Capabilities](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/capabilities.md) [D]; [canva.dev: Requests and responses](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/requests-responses.md) [D]; [Miracamp pricing guide, Aug 2026](https://www.miracamp.com/learn/canva/pricing-plans) [T]
- **Page timing:** not retrieved (video/presentation timing sits with the video researcher).

**Undo and version history**
- **Version history (File > Version history):** restore or "Make a copy" of earlier versions; up to 1,000 previous versions with no time limit; paid users only (free users have no Version History); viewer must be owner or have edit access. — [Help: Review and restore older versions](https://www.canva.com/help/version-history/) [S]
- **Trash:** deleted designs are recoverable for up to 30 days; separate from version history. — [Help: Restore or delete designs from Trash](https://www.canva.com/help/deleted-designs/) [S]
- **Undo/redo depth:** no source retrieved.

**Keyboard shortcuts**
- Shortcut reference page exists (Help Center) and a Design School cheatsheet. Items confirmed: group/ungroup, forward/backward/front, lock (Alt+Shift+L), rulers (Shift+R), text alignment (Shift+Ctrl+L/C/R), and in Presenter view the digits 1 to 9 start a countdown of that many minutes. Total shortcut count not retrieved. — [Help: Canva keyboard shortcuts](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-keyboard-shortcuts/) [S]; [Design School: Canva editor shortcuts cheatsheet](https://www.canva.com/design-school/resources/canva-editor-shortcuts-cheatsheet/) [S]; [Canva Learn: presentation guide](https://www.canva.com/learn/an-easy-guide-to-creating-a-presentation-in-canva/) [S] (the presenter-timer digit shortcut came from a presentations-related search result; the exact page was not pinned)

**Bulk create and data autofill**
- **Bulk Create:** each spreadsheet column is a data field and each row produces one design version; text needs a separate text box per field, images need a frame or grid; multi-page designs need fields connected on each page. Up to 150 data fields can be connected. — [Help: Create designs in bulk](https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create/) [S]
- **Bulk Create limits (conflicting):** main Help page: 300 rows and 60 columns (when using the Bulk Create app); en_au Help page: 300 rows and 150 columns; uploaded spreadsheets (.xlsx/.xls) limited to 500 rows and 52 columns; Canva Sheets has no specific row/column limit. — [Help: Create designs in bulk](https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create/) [S]; [Help (en_au): bulk create / data autofill](https://www.canva.com/en_au/help/bulk-create-data-autofill/) [S]; [Help: Create designs in bulk with Canva Sheets](https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create-from-sheets/) [S]
- **Plan gating:** Bulk Create from Sheets is listed for Pro, Teams, Education and Nonprofit, desktop only; Autofill (apps/API) is on all plans except Free and Pro Lite; connecting custom data sources is for Enterprise admins, developers and brand designers. — [Help: bulk create from Sheets](https://www.canva.com/help/bulk-create-from-sheets/) [S]; [Help: Connect data source apps to autofill](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/data-autofill/) [S]; [Help: Set up designs for Autofill](https://www.canva.com/help/autofill/) [S]
- **Autofill API:** tag template elements as data fields (Bulk Create app > Labels), publish as a brand template, POST /autofills (async job). Needs a plan that includes Autofill (Pro including Education and Nonprofits, Teams, or Enterprise per the guide), MFA on the account, and scopes design:content, design:meta, brandtemplate:meta, brandtemplate:content, asset. Image/video fields accept only uploaded asset IDs. No numeric usage limits are published yet (the guide says limits will be introduced later). — [canva.dev: Autofill designs with data](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/autofill-guide.md) [D]. (This contradicts older reports that the autofill API was Enterprise-only; the guide is undated.)

### Inferences
- (Inference) Hard numbers a clone can mirror directly: gradient up to 10 stops, line-spacing default 1.4 with floor 0.5, letter spacing default 0, alt text 250 characters, 500 uploaded fonts per kit, 18 styles per family, 15 MB font file, minimum canvas 40 px, 1,000-version history.
- (Inference) The public element model has 8 types, while the UI also has frames, grids, charts, QR codes, forms and quizzes; developer-API exposure lags the editor, so the API list is not a complete feature list.
- (Inference) Version history is paid-only and capped at 1,000 versions. A local-first editor storing snapshots in IndexedDB can offer history on every plan, bounded only by browser storage quota.
- (Inference) Canva has no custom margin sizes and no snapping toggle in the retrieved docs; these are small places where Craftly can be more flexible.

### Gaps
- Official count of built-in fonts; text-box features such as auto-fit, text-on-path beyond Curve, drop caps, or OpenType features.
- "Widgets" as an element category; blur as a general element effect; page timing; undo/redo stack depth; autosave behaviour.
- Full keyboard shortcut list and count; Canva's Position panel numeric fields (X/Y/rotation) were not confirmed.
- Reconciliation of 8000 x 3125 vs 8000 x 8000 px canvas limit and of 60 vs 150 column Bulk Create limit.

## 2. Other document types (Docs, Whiteboards, Sheets, Websites, Presentations, Forms, email, print, planner)

### Takeaway
Canva is now a multi-format "Visual Suite": fixed-canvas designs plus Docs (responsive, unlimited length), Whiteboards (unbounded canvas), Sheets, Websites (publish to canva.link or a custom domain), Presentations (Presenter view, Present-and-record, Canva Live), email designs, print products and a social Content Planner. Several of these are server products (hosting, live Q&A, scheduling, printing). Depth of Whiteboards, Docs, Forms and Email could not be retrieved beyond the facts below.

### Cited Findings
- **Design types (developer docs):** Documents, Presentations, Print products (T-shirts, mugs, business cards), Social media content, Videos, Websites, Whiteboards. "Fixed" designs place elements by coordinates; Docs is the only "responsive" type (text-based flow). — [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]
- **Visual Suite 2.0:** introduced at Canva Create 2025 (2025-04-10); lets users move between presentations, videos, whiteboards and websites inside one design; later rollout of "more than 100 updates" to Docs, Sheets, Whiteboards, Presentations and Websites. — [Canva newsroom: Introducing Visual Suite 2.0](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-create-2025/) [S]; [Canva newsroom: Big updates in the Visual Suite](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/visual-suite-100-updates/) [S]; date 2025-04-10 confirmed by [TechCrunch, 2025-04-10](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/10/canva-is-adding-an-ai-assistant-coding-and-sheets-to-its-platform/) [D]
- **Canva Sheets:** spreadsheet with standard features; can import data from HubSpot, Statista and Google Analytics (Data Connectors); "Fill Empty Cells" and Magic Insights are AI-assisted (pointer only). Positioned as an addition to the enterprise offering. — [TechCrunch, 2025-04-10](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/10/canva-is-adding-an-ai-assistant-coding-and-sheets-to-its-platform/) [D]; [Help: Import data into Canva Sheets](https://www.canva.com/help/data-connectors/) [S]
- **Canva Docs:** responsive design type, unlimited length; no margins/bleed/rulers/crop marks; colour themes not available. — [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]; [Help: margins, bleed, rulers](https://www.canva.com/help/margins-bleed-crop-marks/) [S]; element insertion differs (apps use addElementAtCursor in Docs) — [canva.dev: Elements](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/elements.md) [D]
- **Whiteboards:** unbounded width and height. Feature depth (sticky notes, timers, voting) not retrieved. — [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]
- **Websites:** publish to a free domain (canva.link); Free users can have up to 5 live websites on the free domain; Free sites are limited to 10 sections per page, paid plans (Pro, Business, Enterprise, Nonprofits, Education) 20 sections per page; custom domains and unlimited websites need Pro, Teams or Education; Pro, Teams and Business can connect up to 5 existing domains (verification can take up to 72 hours); domains bought via Canva are 1 year and auto-renew; Education gets unlimited sites on the free domain plus 1 existing domain; "Mobile Website (Link in Bio)" templates publish only to canva.link. — [Help: Use your own domains](https://www.canva.com/help/publishing-websites-own-domains/) [S]; [Help: Publish to a free domain](https://www.canva.com/help/publishing-websites-for-free/) [S]; [Help: Purchase domains](https://www.canva.com/help/publishing-websites-purchasing-domains/) [S]; [Help: Canva Websites overview](https://www.canva.com/help/canva-websites/) [S]
- **Presentations:** Present > Presenter view opens a separate presenter window with timer, notes and upcoming-slide preview while the audience sees a clean window; digits 1 to 9 set a countdown in minutes; notes show in "Present and record" too; PPTX export is supported for presentations only (multi-page PPTX export requires a Canva Presentation). — [Canva Learn: creating a presentation](https://www.canva.com/learn/an-easy-guide-to-creating-a-presentation-in-canva/) [S]; [Help: Download designs with notes](https://www.canva.com/help/download-notes/) [S]; [canva.dev: Exports reference](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/rest-apis/reference/exports.md) [D]
- **Canva Live:** audience asks questions and reacts live; the session can only be started from desktop, audience can join from any device; works alongside Presenter view. — [Canva feature page: Canva Live](https://www.canva.com/features/canva-live/) [S]
- **Forms:** forms and quizzes appear as interactive elements in the editor; a standalone "Canva Forms" product was not found. — [Help: Elements](https://www.canva.com/en_in/help/elements/) [S]
- **Email design:** "Canva Email Design" is named in the "Creative Operating System" announcement; email is an editor design type (colour themes unavailable in emails). Canva agreed to acquire Ortto (customer data platform and marketing automation across email, SMS, push, in-app, forms, surveys; 11,000+ customers in 190 countries) on 2026-04-08, tied to "Canva Grow" (create, publish and measure marketing assets). — [Canva newsroom: Creative Operating System](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/creative-operating-system/) [S]; [TechCrunch, 2026-04-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/canva-doubles-down-on-ai-and-marketing-automation-with-simtheory-ortto-acquisitions/) [D]
- **Content Planner (social scheduling):** schedule posts for Facebook Pages, Instagram Business, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Slack, Tumblr; available to Pro, Business, Education and Nonprofit administrators and brand designers; Pro can connect up to 80 social accounts per team; a design goes to one platform at a time; Instagram: 25 scheduled posts per rolling 24 h, up to 20 hashtags, no Creator accounts, no Stories/Reels/galleries, scheduling from desktop browser only (mobile can only view the planner). Pro Lite lacks Content Planner. — [Help: Schedule posts](https://www.canva.com/help/content-planner/) [S]; [Help: Connect to Instagram](https://www.canva.com/help/scheduling-posts-on-instagram/) [S]; [Help: Pro Lite](https://www.canva.com/help/pro-lite/) [S]
- **Print products and Canva Print:** Business plan carries a 10% print discount; print product designs cover T-shirts, mugs and business cards; "Print Shop" was announced at Create 2026 to reshape the Canva Print commerce experience with new products (no product list, prices or dates in the coverage found). — [Canva pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) [S]; [canva.dev: Designs](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/designs.md) [D]; [tutkit.com on Canva Create 2026](https://www.tutkit.com/de/blog/617-canva-create-2026-offline-modus-learn-grid-und-print-shop) [D, weak source]
- **Education "Learn Grid":** announced at Create 2026 as a built-in education hub of ready-to-teach resources for teachers, students and parents; Canva Education is free for students and teachers (South Africa coverage: 320,000+ learners and teachers monthly). — [10and5, 2026-04-21](https://10and5.com/canva-unveils-canva-ai-2-0-launches-offline-mode-and-new-education-platform-for-south-africans/) [D]
- **Pointers only:** Canva Code (interactive "mini apps" and websites with 10M+ MAU as of early 2026, AI-driven) — [TechCrunch, 2026-02-18](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/18/canva-gets-to-4b-in-revenue-as-llm-referral-traffic-rises/) [D]. Affinity (Serif, bought 2024 for about US$380M) relaunched Oct 2025 as one app with a free base version; treat as a photo/vector tool for the other researchers — [Wikipedia: Canva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva) [D].

### Inferences
- (Inference) Features that need no server and could be matched locally: presenter view with notes and timer, notes export, multi-page grid view, interactive-element placeholders (QR). - (Inference) Canva's Docs/Whiteboards/Sheets are separate engines inside one shell, not just design types; a clone would need distinct models for responsive text flow, unbounded boards and tabular data.

- Docs feature list (blocks, comments/suggestions, export formats), Whiteboards tools and limits, Sheets formula/function count and row limits, "Canva Forms" as a product, Canva Email send/analytics details, Canva Print product catalogue/countries/prices, Print Shop and Components specifics. ## 3. Content library and licensing

### Takeaway
Canva's own pricing page (as summarised) lists about 1.6M templates and 4.7M stock photos/graphics on Free versus 3.6M templates and 141M+ premium photos, videos, graphics and audio on Pro. Licensing splits content into Free and Pro tiers; Pro content is licensed per single design and cannot be used as a standalone item or in trademarks. No official font count was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Free plan library:** about 4.7 million stock photos and graphics and 1.6 million templates; Free users cannot edit premium elements in shared designs. — [Canva pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) [S]
- **Pro plan library:** about 141 million premium photos, videos, graphics and audio, and 3.6 million templates (including premium). — [Canva pricing page](https://www.canva.com/pricing/) [S]; same numbers repeated in [Canva Learn: Pro vs Business vs Enterprise](https://www.canva.com/learn/canva-pro-vs-canva-enterprise/) [S]
- **Canva Content License Agreement:** Free Content can be used in more than one design and downloaded stand-alone, but those rights lapse if the design contains any Pro Content. Pro Content is licensed for a single design and cannot be copied, downloaded or distributed as a stand-alone item. Content cannot be used as (part of) a trademark, design mark, trade name, business name or service mark (fonts excepted). ### Takeaway

- (Inference) Canva's VPAT age suggests treating Canva's own accessibility as "good on features, thin on current evidence"; do not use it as a certified benchmark.

### Gaps

## 9. Scale (users, revenue, valuation)

### Takeaway
Latest company-reported figures: about US$4B ARR at end of 2025, 265M+ monthly active users (+20% YoY), 31M+ paid users, and US$500M ARR from accounts with more than 25 seats. Last priced valuation is US$42B (Aug 2025 employee share sale), but TechCrunch reported on 2026-11-04 that early investors had cut it to about US$35B, that growth "has slowed for the first time in a decade", and that Canva is laying off 22% of staff (about 1,271 people); the CEO said growth slowed for the first time in ten years.

- **ARR and users (end of 2025, from COO Cliff Obrecht via TechCrunch):** about US$4B ARR; 265M+ monthly active users, up 20% year over year; 31M+ paid users; accounts with 25+ seats grew 100% to US$500M ARR; Canva's mini-apps/websites AI tool has 10M+ MAU. — [TechCrunch, 2026-02-18](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/18/canva-gets-to-4b-in-revenue-as-llm-referral-traffic-rises/) [D]; same numbers in [TechCrunch, 2026-04-08](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/canva-doubles-down-on-ai-and-marketing-automation-with-simtheory-ortto-acquisitions/) [D]

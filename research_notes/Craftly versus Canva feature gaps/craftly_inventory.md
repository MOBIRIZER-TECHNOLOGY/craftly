## 1. Editor features (text, shapes, lines, frames, charts, tables, QR, drawing, layers, alignment, snapping, guides, grouping, locking, crop, filters, effects, backgrounds, history, pages, resize, find and replace, shortcuts, touch)

### Takeaway
The editor delivers the standard single-user canvas toolset end to end on a custom 2D-canvas renderer (select/move/resize/rotate, rich text, shapes, images with crop and filters, charts, tables, layers, snapping, guides, 120-step undo, version history, multi-page). Its limits are depth rather than breadth: Latin-only text with no weight picker, columns or RTL; no vector pen or boolean operations; shallow tables and charts; groups that cannot be entered; no pinch/two-finger touch gestures; hyperlinks and shape parameters that exist in the data but have no UI.

## 3. Motion: element animations, page transitions, presenter, GIF and WebM export

### Takeaway
Craftly has a complete, dependency-free animation layer: 17 element effects, 8 one-click page animations, 6 page transitions, a full-screen presenter with auto-play, and animated GIF and WebM export driven by the same timeline. It has no timeline editor, keyframes, audio or video footage.

### Cited Findings
- **Element animations (17 presets).** Entrance/exit (11): fade, rise, pan, pop, zoom, wipe, slide, drop, tumble, typewriter, flicker. Emphasis loops (6): breathe, pulse, float, wiggle, spin, flash. Each element has three slots (in, out, loop); direction left/right/up/down for directional presets; duration 0.2-5 s (loops: cycle length), delay 0-10 s (an exit's delay counts back from the page end). - **Page animations (8 presets):** rise, fade, pan, pop, wipe, zoom, drop, cascade; give every non-backdrop, unlocked, visible element the same entrance, staggered bottom-up (stagger 0.1-0.2 s per element, capped at 2 s); elements covering 85 % or more of the page are left still. — [animation.ts:321-346](../../src/core/animation.ts)
- **Page timing and transitions.** Page duration 1-60 s (default 5), "apply to all pages". ### Inferences
- Motion is presentation-grade (slides, stories, social loops), not video editing: no timeline, easing control, keyframes, motion paths, audio or footage (README "Known limitations" says so too).

### Gaps
- Performance of GIF/WebM encoding was not measured by me; the README warns that a 1080p export of a busy design makes the tab less responsive.
- Exact browser coverage of WebM recording beyond "Chrome, Edge or Firefox" (the app's own message) is untested outside Chrome.

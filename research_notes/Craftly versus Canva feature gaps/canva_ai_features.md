## 1. Inventory: what AI features Canva offers (Canva AI / AI 2.0, Magic Studio tools, Visual Suite AI, Affinity, connectors, 2025-2026 additions)

### Takeaway
Canva's AI is now three layers: (a) an agentic assistant, "Canva AI" (v1 Apr 2025, "AI 2.0" Apr 2026), on top of in-house models (Design Model, Craft, Brush, Lucid Origin, Proteus, I2V) plus partner models; (b) the 2023 "Magic Studio" point tools, now mostly absorbed into the Standard/Premium/Ultra allowance system; (c) AI inside every Visual Suite format (Docs, Sheets, Code/Websites, Presentations, Video, Grow) and distributed into other AI products through MCP connectors (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Meta Muse, Google AI Mode) and Affinity. The newest items are Magic Layers (Mar 2026), AI 2.0 (Apr 2026), Craft (Oct 23 2026) and Brush (Oct 30 2026). AI 2.0 is described as a "research preview" at launch and I found no later general-availability announcement.

### Cited Findings

**A. Platform / assistant layer**
- **Canva AI 2.0, announced 2026-04-16 (Canva Create, Los Angeles).** Canva calls it its most significant product evolution since 2013 and the "biggest product launch in our history", "available today as a research preview". Early access went to people who found a "secret password" revealed in the keynote, and "General availability rolls out over the coming weeks". It is powered by the Canva Design Model. Four capabilities: *conversational design*; *agentic orchestration* ("access to Canva's complete design engine, calling the right tools"); *layered object intelligence* (output built from individual editable objects, "ask Canva AI to change something, and only that changes"); *Memory Library*. Six workflows: **Connectors** (Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Notion, Zoom, HubSpot, Microsoft, Atlassian, Linear), **Scheduling** (recurring background tasks, e.g. weekly social batch, morning meeting briefs), **Web research** (structured content dropped into the design), **Brand Intelligence** (apply brand kit or template, bulk-update existing work to the latest brand), **Canva Code 2.0** (HTML import, forms that collect into Sheets, publish to own domain with SSO), **Sheets AI** (budget tracker / timeline / content calendar populated with data). — [OFFICIAL: Canva Newsroom, 2026-04-16](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-create-2026-ai/)
- Preview rollout began to roughly the first one million users on 2026-04-16. — [PRESS: Mediaweek, 2026-04-17](https://www.mediaweek.com.au/canvas-ai-2-0-push-turns-design-tool-into-workplace-rival/)

- **Canva Design Model (2025-10-30).** Canva's first foundation model, trained on its design elements, generating layouts with separately editable layers and objects instead of flat images. Same day: assistant available throughout the UI, `@mention` the bot in comments, **3D object generation**, **style copying**, Canva AI inside Affinity, mini-apps from spreadsheet data, **Canva Grow** (AI ad creation plus MagicBrief analytics), **Forms**, **Email design**. Quote from Canva's head of product: "We started by creating flat images with diffusion models." — [PRESS: TechCrunch, 2025-10-30](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/30/canva-launches-its-own-design-model-adds-new-ai-features-to-the-platform/)
- **Canva Craft (2026-10-23 on Canva's newsroom; TechCrunch and the EU filing say 2026-10-22).** Canva's "first state-of-the-art image generation model", trained from scratch by Canva Research. Two-stage architecture: a *planning model* writes the composition (elements, spatial relations, scale, hierarchy, text content), then a *rendering model* produces up to **2K** across **30+ aspect ratios**; text in **55 languages**; prompts in any language. Output is designed to convert cleanly to layers via Magic Layers ("JSON boundary boxing"). Available now to all Canva users globally in **Dream Lab**, via the **public API**; **MCP support is "Coming Soon"**; "Craft-fast" and "Craft-ultrafast" variants are announced but unreleased. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-23](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/); [PRESS: TechCrunch, 2026-10-22](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/canva-releases-its-first-image-generation-model/)
- **Canva Brush (2026-10-30).** A "vision-language model for design" from Canva Research that edits existing designs ("from a single line of copy to every slide in a deck") using an agentic loop that "writes and runs code to adjust design elements" and inspects the page. Launched to **Canva Pro** subscribers inside Canva AI (select "Brush" in the dropdown); "wider availability coming soon"; Brand Kit support, attachment references and better image-heavy layouts are planned. Claims: up to **69% faster** and **46% lower cost per edit task** than the previous design agent [CLAIM]. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-30](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/)
- **Memory Library / "About Me" page.** Canva AI learns style and preferences from your recent designs and conversations and writes an editable "About Me" Canva Doc. Available on Pro, Teams, Business, Nonprofit. — [OFFICIAL: Help, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/memory-library/)
- **Canva Research** (formed 2025): 160+ scientists and engineers inside a 400-person AI unit; in-house models "already powering dozens of features". — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-22](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/introducing-canva-research/)
- The old Magic Studio landing page (`/magic-studio/`) returns a 404 as of 2026-11-05; the brand survives mainly in Shield and legal copy and in tool names. — [OFFICIAL: canva.com/magic-studio, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/magic-studio/)

**B. Magic Studio point tools (launched 2023-10-04, with Canva Shield) and later siblings**
- **Launch list (2023-10-04):** Magic Design (prompt or uploaded media becomes designs, videos with auto music and Beat Sync, presentations), Resize & Magic Switch, Magic Grab, Magic Expand, Magic Morph, Magic Edit, Magic Media (Text to Image plus short video from text or image), Magic Animate, Magic Write with built-in brand voice, plus partner apps in the app marketplace. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2023-10-04](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/magic-studio/)
- **Magic Design.** Prompt (or text, images, ideas) becomes a first-draft design. Help states it *does not generate video from scratch*: it arranges and styles content, and generated clips come from Magic Media or Canva AI video. Magic Design for Video supports 1920x1080, 1080x1920 and 1080x1080. Today it is reached through "Canva AI" / "Generate a design with Canva AI" (a Premium tool). — [OFFICIAL: Help "Use Magic Design" (excerpt in the safe-use page), read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-studio-safely-and-legally/); [OFFICIAL: Help "Understanding your AI usage", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/)
- **Magic Write (+ Brand Voice).** Launched 2022-12-07; "more than one billion words" by Oct 2023. Quick actions: generate from prompt, Rewrite, Fix spelling, Continue writing, Shorten, More fun / More formal, Change voice, Create Summary (also summarises sticky notes and shapes). **Brand Voice**: guidelines capped at **500 characters**, 21 languages, only for Pro, Business, Teams, Enterprise, Education and Nonprofits; only owners, admins and brand designers can edit. — [OFFICIAL: Help "Use Magic Write", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-write/); [OFFICIAL: Help "Brand voice", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/brand-voice/); [PRESS: Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva)
- **Magic Edit.** Brush, click-to-select or "All", plus a text prompt to add, replace or restyle (day-to-night, change object colour). Official limitations: lighting/colour/style may not match, results can be empty or unexpected, the edited region is more pixelated than the rest; avoid flags, text, faces, hands, celebrities and brands; thin brush strokes can return empty results; add one thing at a time. — [OFFICIAL: Help "Use Magic Edit" (excerpt in safe-use page), read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-studio-safely-and-legally/)
- **Magic Eraser.** Brush, click, or "Foreground" selection, then Erase. "Improved removal quality" shipped in the May 2026 release notes. — [OFFICIAL: Help, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/magic-eraser/); [3P: Releasebot Canva changelog (May 27 2026 entry)](https://releasebot.io/updates/canva)
- **Magic Grab.** Select a subject so it becomes a movable/resizable element while the background is kept. Not supported for images inside frames or grids. Works in Docs, Video and the Photo Editor. — [OFFICIAL: Help, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-grab/)
- **Magic Expand (outpainting).** "Goes beyond the border": turn a vertical shot into horizontal, recover what is outside the frame. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2023-10-04](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/magic-studio/)
- **Magic Switch / Resize / Translate.** One click reformats a design into another size or doc type (deck to summary doc or blog post), and translates it. "Translate" uses "advanced machine learning"; the help page lists about **137 language entries** by my count (Asia 59, Europe 45, Africa 24, Oceania 9). A separate **Smartling** app routes designs to human or TMS translation. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2023-10-04](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/magic-studio/); [OFFICIAL: Help "How translations work", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/translation-languages/); [OFFICIAL: Help "Translate using Smartling", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/translate-using-smartling/)
- **Magic Morph.** Prompt-driven effects and textures on text or shapes (e.g. seasonal themes) with auto-applied consistent style. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2023-10-04](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/magic-studio/)
- **Magic Animate.** One click: AI picks an animation style for the whole design (Recommended/Alternative styles). Only for Presentation, Social and Video design types. — [OFFICIAL: Help (excerpt in safe-use page), read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-studio-safely-and-legally/)

**C. Magic Layers (2026)**
- Announced 2026-03-10/11: turns a flat image into an editable multi-layer design with live text and separated objects; public beta in the US, UK, Canada and Australia at launch. On 2026-06-07 it reached **Gemini and ChatGPT** ("available to all users"). Canva Research says a *learned decomposition model plus a large font library* produces editable, format-ready text rather than pixels, and calls it "one of Canva's fastest-growing AI tools". Premium tool; input single-page PNG/JPG up to 5000 px on the longest edge [snippet only]; multi-page PDFs and vectors unsupported. — [3P: Releasebot (Mar 10 and Jun 7 2026 entries)](https://releasebot.io/updates/canva); [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-22](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/introducing-canva-research/); [OFFICIAL: Help, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/editable-magic-layers/); [PRESS: PetaPixel, 2026-03-11 (snippet only; page 403)](https://petapixel.com/2026/03/11/canvas-new-ai-tool-turns-flat-images-into-editable-layers)

## 2. Per-feature access: plans, credits and limits, commercial-use and indemnification terms

### Takeaway
Since 2026 Canva meters AI with one monthly "AI allowance" shared across Standard, Premium and Ultra tools. A set of "AI design tools" (Translate, Magic Write, Background Remover, AI Voice, Beat Sync, Enhance Voice, animations) is outside the allowance under fair use. Free gets limited Standard/Premium and no Ultra; Pro gets 10x Free; Business and Enterprise 20x; AI Pass ($100 per person per month) multiplies this to 40x/20x of the base plan. Users own outputs and may use them commercially at their own risk; Canva gives no warranty and only eligible Enterprise customers (over 100 seats per Canva's AI page) get IP indemnification.

## 4. Trust and safety: moderation, labelling/watermarks, data use and training, Canva Shield

### Takeaway
Canva Shield is the umbrella: automated prompt/output moderation, reporting, admin controls, a creator-compensation programme and enterprise-only IP indemnification. AI-generated outputs carry C2PA-style "AI-generated content metadata" when a feature creates or significantly alters content (not for editing tools like Background Remover), and the AI terms forbid stripping it; I found no visible watermark. On training data: individual users control it in Privacy Settings, Education is never used, Business/Teams/Enterprise are locked off pending an admin opt-in, creators are paid and can opt out; the privacy policy explicitly permits training on account content subject to those controls.

### Cited Findings
- **Moderation:** automatic moderation of certain input prompts (medical, political, hateful, explicit), ML scanning of generated outputs "before the result is made visible", a user feedback/report loop, blocked terms, human oversight, and safety reviews of all generative products. Users must not bypass filters. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom "Canva Shield", updated 2026-01-05](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/safe-ai-canva-shield/); [OFFICIAL: Canva Shield page, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/safe-ai-canva-shield/)
- **Fairness work:** a debiasing effort started on Text to Image, with an in-house "bias steering model" promised for open-sourcing (announced 2023, text retained in the 2026 update). — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-01-05](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/safe-ai-canva-shield/)
- **Labelling / provenance:** the AI Product Terms (effective 2026-06-26) prohibit using AI Products to "mislead anyone that the content generated by AI Products is human-generated" and to "remove, alter, or disable any provenance or metadata tags from any AI-generated content, including but not limited to C2PA metadata". Canva's Background Remover help says Canva "only adds AI-generated content metadata when a feature creates entirely new content or significantly changes it, like Magic Media or Magic Edit", and that Background Remover (an editing tool) does not trigger "AI content" labels on platforms such as Instagram or TikTok. Help also asks users to disclose AI assistance as best practice. — [OFFICIAL: AI Product Terms](https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/); [OFFICIAL: Help "Background Remover", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/background-remover/); [OFFICIAL: Help "safely and legally"](https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-studio-safely-and-legally/)
- **Watermarks:** no visible watermark policy for Canva AI outputs found in any Canva page I opened. Whether Veo 3 clips carry Google's invisible SynthID in Canva is unconfirmed. A search aggregator said Canva reportedly embeds C2PA credentials (secondary). — [3P: AIProductivity (snippet only)](https://aiproductivity.ai/news/ai-content-labeling-synthid-c2pa-expansion/)
- **Data use, individuals:** Canva "won't use your user content to improve AI-powered features unless this is consistent with your Privacy Settings"; improvement draws on creator content (paid, opt-out), licensed stock/partner content, openly licensed content, and user content and general usage data *per privacy settings*. ### Inferences
- Canva's labelling rule is feature-based, not content-based: "creates or significantly changes content" is its line between generative and editing tools, which is also useful evidence for Section 6.

- Enterprise indemnity is the only commercial-risk transfer; everyone else carries IP risk, consistent with analyst calls for provenance and indemnification (Futurum).

## 6. Which features are genuinely generative and which are classical / discriminative

### Takeaway
Canva itself draws the line in two places: its AI-labelling rule (metadata only when a feature "creates entirely new content or significantly changes it") and its allowance tiers. Background Remover, Beat Sync and Enhance Voice are perception/signal-processing models, not generators. Everything that produces new pixels, text, audio or code is generative. A few (Magic Grab, Magic Layers, Upscale, Magic Eraser) are hybrids.

### Cited Findings
- **Canva-stated "editing, not generation":** Background Remover "uses AI, but it's considered an editing tool", is "not a content generator", and does not add AI-generated-content metadata. The technology originated with the Vienna lab Kaleido (2019, acquired 2021). — [OFFICIAL: Help "Background Remover", read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/help/background-remover/); [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-22](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/introducing-canva-research/)
- **Canva-stated "AI to find structure":** Beat Sync "uses AI technology to find the beats in a song", then snaps cuts to beat markers. Enhance Voice is "AI-powered" noise removal. — [OFFICIAL: Help "Syncing audio with video"](https://www.canva.com/help/syncing-audio-with-video/); [OFFICIAL: Help "Enhance Voice"](https://www.canva.com/help/enhance-voice/)
- **Canva-stated generation:** Magic Media (images, graphics, video, 3D), Dream Lab / Craft (two-stage planner + renderer), Create a Video Clip (Veo 3), Image to Video (generative video model), Magic Edit (prompt-driven edits; labelled as AI-generated content), Music/SFX generator, AI Voice, Magic Write and Brand Voice, Canva Code (LLM code generation), Sheets AI / Magic Insights / Magic Charts / Magic Formulas, Docs AI, presenter-notes generator, 3D and shape generators, Magic Background. — [OFFICIAL: Help pages cited in Section 1](https://www.canva.com/help/ai-access/)
- **Decomposition / structured prediction (Canva-described):** Magic Layers is a "learned decomposition model" that outputs editable layers and live text from a flat image; Craft's "JSON boundary boxing" makes this faster and more accurate. Affinity's "Scene Decomposition" and Cavalry's announced "Image to Layers" are the same class. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-22](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/introducing-canva-research/); [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-23](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/); [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-09-16](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-prosuite-launch/)
- **Agentic / planning (Canva-described):** Brush "writes and runs code to adjust design elements" and inspects the page; AI 2.0 calls Canva's design tools and returns drafts. — [OFFICIAL: Newsroom, 2026-10-30](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-brush/); [PRESS: TechCrunch, 2026-04-16](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/16/canvas-ai-assistant-can-now-call-various-tools-to-make-designs-for-you/)
- **Allowance tiers as a cost proxy:** lightweight tools (Upscale, Magic Formulas) are Standard; generation of images, designs, code, audio and Image to Video is Premium; conversational design and Veo video are Ultra; Background Remover, Beat Sync, AI Voice, animations and Enhance Voice are outside the allowance. — [OFFICIAL: Pricing and Help, read 2026-11-05](https://www.canva.com/pricing/)
- **Classification of the remaining tools (my inference from the feature descriptions; Canva does not label them):** see Inferences.

### Inferences (my classification; confidence in brackets)
- **Classical / discriminative (perception, segmentation, signal processing):** Background Remover, including video [high]; Beat Sync (beat tracking) [high]; Enhance Voice (neural denoising/enhancement) [medium]; template search/recommendations (Canva's AI work began there in 2017) [high]; Magic Animate's style choice (recommendation, possibly rule-based) [low].
- **Hybrid (a perception step plus a fill/generation step):** Magic Grab (subject segmentation, then background kept, which likely needs inpainting) [medium]; Magic Eraser (selection plus inpainting; "Erase or retouch") [medium]; Image Upscaler (learned super-resolution, a restoration model that hallucinates detail; Canva warns about faces and text) [medium]; Magic Layers (decomposition model, with possible fill of occluded areas) [medium].
- **Genuinely generative (diffusion / LLM / video / audio):** Craft, Dream Lab, Magic Media, Magic Edit, Magic Expand (outpainting), Magic Morph, Magic Background, Style Match, 3D, shapes, Image to Video, Create a Video Clip, Music/SFX, AI Voice, Magic Write / Brand Voice, Docs/Sheets AI, Canva Code, Canva AI 2.0 agent, Brush, Translate (neural machine translation, generative text) [high for named ones; medium for Morph, Style Match, Translate].
- **Agentic orchestration** (AI 2.0, Brush, scheduling, connectors) is a software layer over the above rather than a separate model type.
- Implication for Craftly: the discriminative tools (background removal, subject selection, beat/audio analysis) are the cheapest to reproduce locally in a browser; the generative tools depend on model weights Craftly would have to bundle or call.

### Gaps
- Canva does not publish architectures for Magic Eraser, Magic Grab, Magic Expand, Magic Morph, Upscale or Magic Animate, so the hybrid and classical labels above are inferences, not citations.
- Whether Background Remover is still the Kaleido model or has been replaced.
- Auto-captions (speech-to-text) were not found in the sources I could open.

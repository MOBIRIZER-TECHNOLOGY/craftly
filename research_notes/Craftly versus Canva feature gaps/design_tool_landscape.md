## Key Question 1: Who are the main competitors, and how do they compare (positioning, target users, AI, video, collaboration, pricing, openness, strengths/weaknesses, recent releases)?

### Takeaway
Canva (265M MAU, ~$4B ARR) still defines the category but looks stressed in H2 2026 (growth outlook cut to ~20%, 22% of staff laid off, free AI allowance cut 10x), while Adobe Express (Firefly credits, bundling, Embed SDK) and Figma (agentic platform, AI credits, 43% growth) attack from either side. Below them the field splits into credit-metered AI-first template tools (Kittl, Visme, Pixlr, Gamma), free pro-grade native tools (Affinity, Penpot), browser tools that process files locally (Photopea), video-first tools (CapCut, Clipchamp, Descript, Google Vids) and a very fast-moving wave of AI-written open-source Canva clones (Doory ~2,250 GitHub stars within 26 days of creation).

## Key Question 2: Which embeddable and open-source building blocks exist (Polotno, IMG.LY CE.SDK, Adobe Express Embed SDK, Canva APIs, Fabric/Konva/Pixi editors, open-source Canva clones) and how mature are they?

## Key Question 4: What is table stakes in 2026 for a design editor, and what remains a differentiator?

### Takeaway
Table stakes are now: large template libraries, brand kit, one-click resize, background remover, multi-format export, real-time sharing/comments, mobile/tablet access, video/animation export, and an AI assistant with credits - and "offline" and "free pro-grade core" have just been conceded by Canva (Offline, free Affinity). Differentiators are shifting to agent/MCP operability, typographic/layout precision, template quality and ecosystem, open formats and privacy guarantees, and unmetered local features.

### Cited Findings
- Templates are the stated moat: Adobe Express Free advertises 100,000+ templates; VistaCreate 200K+; Doory ships 10 and HN commenters say the real value is high-quality built-in templates and that Canva's edge is its IP plus ecosystem (templates, assets, stock photography). [V/U] — [adobe.com/express/pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing), [create.vista.com](https://create.vista.com/), [HN 50049872](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049872)
- Brand kit is universal and typically gated to paid tiers: Adobe Express Premium+, Visme Business+, Canva (Brand Intelligence in AI 2.0), VistaCreate; open-source clones also ship it (Doory, HyCanvas). [V] — [adobe.com/express/pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing), [visme.co/pricing](https://www.visme.co/pricing/), [github.com/shaongitbd/doory](https://github.com/shaongitbd/doory)
- Resize and background removal are gated as "premium/AI tools" by incumbents (Adobe Express Premium: one-click Resize, Remove Video Background; Canva Pro bullet "resize, translate, remove background" -> "AI tools") but are shipped free in open-source editors (Doory: resize to another format, in-browser background removal; HyCanvas: Magic Resize + background removal). [V/T] — [adobe.com/express/pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing), [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/canva/diffs/2026W38), [Doory](https://github.com/shaongitbd/doory), [HyCanvas](https://github.com/hyscaler/HyCanvas)
- Collaboration: real-time co-editing, comments, version history and share links are expected in new entrants (Doory, HyCanvas, Beautiful.ai Team, Penpot, Figma). [V] — [Doory](https://github.com/shaongitbd/doory), [HyCanvas](https://github.com/hyscaler/HyCanvas), [beautiful.ai/pricing](https://www.beautiful.ai/pricing)
- Video/animation: Canva, Adobe Express (2 h video on Premium), VistaCreate, Kittl (AI video Feb 2026), Doory (27 enter/exit animations + 12 emphasis + 16 transitions + MP4/WebM/GIF), HyCanvas (multi-track timeline, captions, green-screen keying). [V] — sources above
- AI: every major tool meters AI by credits/tokens (Adobe Express 250/mo on Premium, Figma 3,000/mo per Full seat, Pixlr 80-10,000, Visme 10-500, Kittl 100-12,000 non-rolling tokens, Descript 100-1,500, Canva "AI usage" with premium multipliers) and is moving to conversational/agentic creation (Canva AI 2.0, Adobe Firefly AI Assistant, Figma agent, Gamma). [V/T] — see KQ1 links
- Offline and "free pro tool" are no longer unique: Canva Offline (14 days, free) and Affinity free forever; but both retain a Canva account requirement and cloud sync. [I] — [India TV News](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/canva-offline-mode-lets-users-create-and-edit-designs-without-internet-for-up-to-14-days-2026-06-11-1044508), [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/11/02/canva-free-affinity-app-ipad/)
- Mobile/tablet: Affinity for iPad (2026-11-02), Canva Offline on mobile, Canvix claims desktop/tablet/mobile; Figma/Adobe/CapCut have native apps. [I/V] — [MacRumors](https://macrumors.com/2026/11/02/canva-free-affinity-app-ipad/), [Canvix](https://github.com/mir-ashiq/canvix)
- Agent/MCP operability is emerging as expected: Canva MCP server, Figma MCP (usage 3x QoQ), Penpot MCP, Polotno App MCP, OpenCut roadmap MCP, IMG.LY CoDesign SDK; an HN commenter argues credible Canva alternatives must be API- and MCP-first. [V/U] — [canva.dev](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/llms.txt), [Figma 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001579878/000162828026070133/q326pressrelease.htm), [Polotno App](https://github.com/polotno-project/polotno-studio-app), [HN 50049872](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049872)
- Print/interop: IMG.LY (PDF/X, CMYK, bleed, PPTX/PSD/AI/IDML import), Doory (bleed + crop marks), Affinity (PSD/AI/INDD import, .af), HyCanvas and Presenton (editable PPTX) set expectations for import/export fidelity. [V] — [img.ly](https://img.ly/products/creative-sdk), [Doory](https://github.com/shaongitbd/doory), [Wikipedia: Affinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_%28software%29)
- Accessibility: IMG.LY WCAG AA; Doory high-contrast mode. [V] — as above
- Quality complaints by experienced users about Canva itself (alignment, text rendering, kerning) indicate typography/layout precision is an open differentiator. [U] — [HN 50049872 (flexagoon comment)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049872)

### Inferences
Proposed classification for a "world-class" 2026 editor (analysis, not sourced fact):
- **Hygiene (absence disqualifies)**: layered multi-page canvas with snapping/guides/rulers, rich text + fonts (incl. user fonts), shapes/icons/stickers, image crop/mask/filters, undo/redo, export PNG/JPG/SVG/PDF, hundreds of quality templates by use case, one-click resize to formats, brand kit, background remover, responsive/tablet-capable UI, keyboard accessibility.
- **Expected at parity with Canva/Adobe Free**: video/GIF/MP4 export with basic animation, present mode, shareable links/comments, free stock/icon library, QR codes/charts, PPTX/PSD import, print bleed/crop marks, offline use.
- **Paid-tier expectation (credits)**: generative image/edit/expand, text AI, conversational "agent" creation, brand governance/locking, approvals, API/MCP.
- **Differentiators with evidence of demand**: no-account/no-upload privacy guarantee (Photopea model; Affinity's claim that it cannot access local files is undermined by its Canva account requirement), open file format and exportability (.polotno JSON, .af), unmetered on-device AI (bg removal, upscaling), MCP/agent control of the same document, typography precision, curated template quality, Penpot-style self-host/open-source credibility.

### Gaps
- No independent survey of which features users value most; table-stakes list is inferred from vendor feature gating and OSS clone READMEs.
- Mobile app parity (Canva/Adobe native apps) and accessibility conformance of incumbents were not verified.

## Key Question 5: Where can a privacy-first, local-first, offline, possibly open-source editor differentiate; who won that way; what are the risks?

### Takeaway
There is verified demand and precedent for local/open design tools (Photopea, Penpot, Affinity, Excalidraw, OpenCut, Obsidian), and incumbents have just exposed AI cost and pricing pain; but "offline" and "free" were partly neutralised by Canva in 2026, AI-written clones have collapsed the cost of copying code, and the true moats (templates, assets, trust, distribution) must be built deliberately. Local-first collaboration and sync remain unsolved-hard.

### Cited Findings
- Local-first definition (7 ideals): no spinners; work not trapped on one device; network optional; seamless collaboration; the Long Now; security and privacy by default; user retains ultimate ownership; proposes CRDTs. [I] — [Ink & Switch, Apr 2019](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/local-first/)
- Photopea: files stay local (no upload unless the user chooses), ad-supported + Premium, one developer, ~ $1M revenue in 2020, still prominent in 2026 (HN threads Oct 2026). [W/U] — [Wikipedia: Photopea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopea), [HN 49972730](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49972730)
- Penpot: open-source/self-host drove a signup surge after Adobe-Figma (+5,600% growth rate, on-prem +400%) and 62k GitHub stars by 2026; HN commenters praise on-prem and pricing; the CEO framed open source as letting developers hack, self-host and extend the tool. [I/U] — [TechCrunch 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/27/penpot-inks-8m-as-signups-for-its-open-source-spin-on-figma-jump-5600-after-adobes-20b-acquisition-move), [HN 46064757](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46064757)
- Affinity's free-forever pivot comes with a vendor statement that content is not used to train AI and that Affinity cannot access local files (quoted in a MacRumors forum post), yet it coexists with a mandatory Canva account and an AI paywall - commentators called it freemium and noted the tension. [V/U] (snippet) — [MacRumors forums](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/canva-relaunches-affinity-as-free-all-in-one-design-app.2470192); [W] — [Wikipedia: Affinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_%28software%29)
- Open-source/local video wedge: OpenCut reached 97k stars in ~16 months as an open-source CapCut alternative (and 447-pt HN launch), though early HN comments flagged ease-of-use gaps versus CapCut and suspicion about star counts; it is now rewriting from scratch. [D/U] — [GitHub OpenCut-app/OpenCut](https://github.com/OpenCut-app/OpenCut), [HN 44553752](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44553752)
- Local/BYOK AI design: open-codesign (8,162 stars, MIT, BYOK, Ollama, "local-first"), Presenton (11,716 stars, Ollama/LM Studio), Polotno App (local JSON + local MCP) show active build-out of local-first AI design stacks. [D] — [open-codesign](https://github.com/opencoworkai/open-codesign), [Presenton](https://github.com/presenton/presenton), [Polotno App](https://github.com/polotno-project/polotno-studio-app)
- Enterprise privacy as a sales feature: IMG.LY says no image, video or personal data is sent to its servers and offers air-gapped deployments; Penpot Private Server at $50k/yr; Polotno sends design content to its servers only for Cloud Render/AI. [V] — [img.ly/pricing](https://img.ly/pricing), [penpot.app/pricing](https://penpot.app/pricing), [polotno.com/pricing](https://polotno.com/pricing)
- Incumbents' AI economics create an opening: Canva's AI delivery costs forced a growth-outlook cut and slower rollout; free-tier AI cut to 20 uses; Canva AI 2.0 connectors pull data from Slack/Gmail/Drive/Calendar (more data egress, not less). [I/T] — [Fortune 2026-08-12](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/canva-startup-growth-ai-costs-revenue-forecast-by-third/), [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/canva/diffs/2026W38), [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/?p=739587)
- Monetisation precedents without lock-in: Photopea ads + premium + self-hosted licence; Obsidian paid Sync/Publish on a local-first core; Penpot hosted tiers + enterprise; Polotno/IMG.LY SDK licences; Affinity free core + Canva upsell. [W/V] — [Photopea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Photopea), [Obsidian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obsidian_%28software%29), [penpot.app/pricing](https://penpot.app/pricing)
- Risk - cloning cost: Doory (Claude-authored, 6 commits/3 days) and Photosuite (Photopea derivative) show LLMs can reproduce browser apps quickly; an HN commenter argued any JavaScript site is now effectively in LLM training sets and can be trivially rewritten. [U] — [HN 50049872](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049872), [HN 49972730](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49972730)
- Risk - maturity/trust: HN scepticism toward young OSS clones (unsubstantiated README claims on Doory, a commenter asking where Avnac's LICENSE file was at launch, star-count suspicion for OpenCut). [U] — [HN 47897638](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47897638), [HN 44553752](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44553752)
- Risk - scale performance: Penpot self-hosters report crashes/lag with large files and one commenter says it slows badly on large canvases. [U] — [HN 46064757](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46064757)
- Risk - competitors moving into the slot: Canva Offline (free, 14 days), free Affinity on iPad/desktop, Polotno's free local-first desktop app with MCP, Remotion/OpenCut for video. [I/V] — links above
- Risk - licensing of ML assets: the most popular browser background-removal package is AGPL-3.0 and Bria's RMBG models carry a non-standard Hugging Face licence tag; permissive options exist (BiRefNet MIT, MODNet Apache-2.0, rembg MIT). [D] — links in KQ2

### Inferences
- The strongest wedge appears to be "the Canva-like editor you own": no account, no upload, no credits, open format, works fully offline (Canva Offline needs pre-marking and expires after 14 days; Affinity needs a Canva account; Polotno App still pulls assets/fonts from network).
- Because code is cheap to clone, sustainable differentiation is likely in: curated templates and assets with clear licences, precise typography/layout engine quality, on-device AI that is free to use (no marginal cost for Craftly), trust signals (open source, auditable no-network claim, privacy statement), and an agent/MCP interface to the local document.
- Collaboration is the structural weak point of a no-backend architecture (CRDT/sync complexity per Ink & Switch; Doory/HyCanvas offer collab only via a self-hosted server); a credible path is optional end-to-end-encrypted sync or peer-to-peer later, with single-user/offline excellence first.
- Monetisation needs a deliberate model (Photopea ads/premium, Obsidian paid sync/publish, SDK/OEM licensing like Polotno/IMG.LY, pro template/asset packs) since there is no account/credit funnel.

### Gaps
- No quantitative evidence that privacy/local-first alone drives purchase in design (only anecdotal HN/Trustpilot signals); no independent conversion/retention data for Photopea, Penpot or Affinity.
- Browser storage limits/eviction behaviour for IndexedDB-based persistence, File System Access API support across browsers, and WebGPU/WebCodecs support for local video/AI were not researched here (adjacent topics).
- Whether the market will pay for a free/local-first Canva alternative (willingness-to-pay) is untested; Obsidian/Photopea are analogues, not proof.

## Key Question 6: What do users complain about in the incumbents (pricing changes, AI credits, privacy/training on content, performance)?

### Takeaway
Evidence clusters around price volatility and AI metering (Canva Teams +300% in 2024; 2026 Pro price oscillation and free-AI cut; credit/token allowances everywhere), support and account friction, long-memory privacy distrust, and quality/performance gripes from professionals; however sampling is limited (Reddit/G2/Capterra not accessible) and Canva's own overall Trustpilot score remains 4.0/5.

### Cited Findings
- Pricing shock: Canva Teams up to +300% (Sep 2024); users threatened to leave; a UX designer warned it could price out smaller teams. [I] — [Fortune 2024-09-03](https://www.fortune.com/2024/09/03/canva-hiking-teams-subscription-prices-ai-features); (snippet) Fox Business coverage of backlash over the increases and a reported later walk-back — [Fox Business](https://foxbusiness.com/technology/online-graphic-design-platform-hit-backlash-over-insane-price-hikes-reach-300), [Dead Pixels Society](https://thedeadpixelssociety.com/canva-walks-back-controversial-price-hike/)
- 2026 price/AI churn at Canva: Pro $144 -> $180 -> $144 within ~6 weeks (Jul-Sep 2026) and free-tier AI cut from 200 Standard uses to 20; plans re-labelled from "AI allowance" to "AI usage" with premium multipliers. [T] — [PricingSaaS W32](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/canva/diffs/2026W32), [W38](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/canva/diffs/2026W38)
- Credit/token scarcity as a recurring friction: Kittl tokens do not roll over and cost $16 per 2,000 top-up; Adobe Express Premium 250 credits/mo vs Firefly Pro 4,000; Pixlr/Visme/Descript tiered credits; Figma sells AI credits by seat type (credit monetisation reportedly began 2026-03-18); Affinity's gen-AI behind Canva premium drew complaints. [V/I] — [We Are Founders](https://www.wearefounders.uk/kittl-pricing-2026-every-plan-explained/), [adobe.com/express/pricing](https://www.adobe.com/express/pricing), [figma.com/pricing](https://www.figma.com/pricing/), [Tedium](https://tedium.co/2025/10/30/canva-affinity-free-loss-leader-strategy/)

[U] — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/canva.com)
- Privacy/security history: Canva's 2019 breach (~139M users) and 2020 password-cracking incident; CapCut's 2023 class action over biometric/geolocation data (mostly dismissed Sep 2025); Affinity critics feared user projects might train ML (Affinity says no). [W] — [Wikipedia: Canva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva), [Wikipedia: CapCut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CapCut), [Wikipedia: Affinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affinity_%28software%29)
- AI-training defaults: third-party summaries conflict on whether Canva Free/Pro content-for-AI-improvement toggles are on by default; Teams/Business/Enterprise/Education are reported as excluded; creators can opt out of template training; third-party apps inside Canva fall outside the privacy settings. (snippet; Canva's own page 403) — [Redact.dev summary](https://redact.dev/blog/canva-ai-training-opt-out), [Canva privacy page](https://www.canva.com/en_in/trust/privacy/)
- Quality/performance: one Hacker News user (2026-10-12) rated Canva among the worst graphic editors they have used, citing difficult alignment, poor text rendering and bad template kerning (single opinion); Penpot users report slowness/crashes on large files and heavy RAM use. [U] — [HN 50049872](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049872), [HN 46064757](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46064757)
- Adobe: HN "I managed to unsubscribe from Adobe CC without being charged" (2026-07-06) suggests Adobe cancellation friction remains a live topic (26 pts). [U] — [HN 48807269](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48807269)
- Stability of the vendor itself: Canva's 22% layoff and its CEO's admission that growth slowed for the first time in a decade create vendor-continuity worry for team customers; Canva also slowed AI rollout because of cost. [I] — [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-11-04/canva-restructure-job-losses/107344304), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/canva-startup-growth-ai-costs-revenue-forecast-by-third/)

### Inferences
- The consistent user pain points that a local-first product can credibly address are: unpredictable pricing/credit limits (no metering for local features), data-egress/privacy distrust (no network, no account), vendor lock-in/continuity (open format, works if the company disappears - Ink & Switch "Long Now"), and cancellation/support friction (no subscription for the core).
- Pain points a local-first product will NOT solve by default: template breadth/quality, stock content, real-time team collaboration, high-end generative AI quality, and large-document browser performance.

### Gaps
- Reddit (r/canva, r/graphic_design), G2, Capterra and Product Hunt review sampling failed; no systematic sentiment analysis; Trustpilot sample limited to the first page of reviews.
- No primary-source confirmation of Adobe's 2024 terms-of-service controversy, Figma's AI-training policy, or Canva's current default AI-training settings was obtained.
- The Canva 2024 price-hike "walk-back" is only confirmed by headline snippets.
